Project Toolkit

The 11 modules included in the Day Trader Skill project.

Chart Scanner

Scans many charts for setups such as opening-range breakouts, flags and oversold bounces.

News Monitor

Tracks breaking news on the stocks you watch and flags items that may move the price.

Entry Timer

Watches a setup bar by bar and reports when the entry condition is triggered.

Exit Planner

Sets a stop and a target before entry, so the exit plan is defined in advance.

Time Machine

Simulates possible outcomes of a trade from past price data before any money is risked.

Risk Manager

Checks each trade against limits such as max loss, daily loss and position size, returning PASS or BLOCK.

Budget Manager

Sizes each position to your budget and your maximum loss per trade.

Order Executor

Prepares orders for your broker. Paper trading by default, and nothing is sent without your explicit confirmation.

Price Alerts

Alerts you when a price, stop or target is reached.

Earnings Analyst

Reads company earnings reports and summarizes what changed.

Pelosi Tracker

Reads publicly filed congressional stock-trade disclosures as they are published.

About the Project

Day Trader Skill is an open-source project (MIT license) written in Python. It uses publicly available data such as Yahoo Finance charts, Nasdaq Trader symbol lists, SEC EDGAR filings and the House Clerk's disclosures.

Nothing in this project can place an order on its own. Every trade needs your explicit approval, and paper trading is the default.

This website is an informational project page. It does not provide personalized financial advice or guarantee results. Market data can be delayed, inaccurate or incomplete. Always do your own research before making any trading decision.
